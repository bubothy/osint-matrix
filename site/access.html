how the check runs
01 · owner

Owners come from OWNER_IDS. They skip approval and maintenance, and cannot be locked out by the rate limiter.

02 · approved

IDs in the allow-list table. Everything except owner commands, within the rate limit.

03 · refused

Any other ID, or an approved ID while maintenance is on. Gets your note, no data, and an audit row.
